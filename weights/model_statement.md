# Model release statement

**Manuscript status.** The accompanying paper is **in revision**. Public details of the trained ALLocate models (architecture choices, training regime, evaluation, and any planned release) will be aligned with the **published manuscript** and supplementary materials.

**Please wait.** We are **not** publishing downloadable checkpoints or endorsing use of unreleased weights in this repository until release terms are finalized. **Do not** treat this folder as an offer of production-ready models for clinical or regulatory use.

## What this means for this repository

- **Checkpoints** (for example under `base/`, `allocate/`, or paths referenced by training code) are **not** guaranteed to be present, current, or licensed for redistribution.
- **Release**—including hosting, file naming, checksums, licensing, and documentation—requires **institutional and partner approval** and will be announced here when available.
- For **data** use and cohorts, see [`data/availability_statement.md`](../data/availability_statement.md).
